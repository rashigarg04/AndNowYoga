Your Teacher

Who Will You Be Talking To?

Ram Sahu — Classical Hatha Yoga Teacher

"I spent five years in the corporate world before making a decision in 2016 to dedicate myself to yoga."

He'd built a career after his MBA — the kind most people spend their twenties working toward. And then, in 2016, he left it. Not out of frustration with the work, but because something else had started asking for his attention.

That decision led him to the Isha School of Hatha Yoga in Coimbatore, where he trained intensively — not for a weekend, or a certificate, but for 1,750 hours. The kind of training that changes how you see practice altogether.

He's been teaching Classical Hatha Yoga ever since. Eight years now, and counting.

Yogic Perspective

Yoga isn't just something you do.
It gradually changes the way you experience yourself.

It starts with the body — how you sit, how you breathe, how you hold your attention. Slowly, it moves into the mind, and then into the way you live each day. Classical Hatha Yoga was never meant to be a workout. It's a gradual, deliberate process, and every conversation here begins from that understanding.
