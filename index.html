Why Practitioners Plateau in Their Sadhana

Many dedicated practitioners practice diligent sadhana for years without experiencing deep inner transformation. Here is why many find themselves stuck on a plateau:

Micro-Misalignments

Slight physical inaccuracies in posture (Asana) that accumulate tension over time instead of freeing prana.

Superficial Breath Awareness

Practicing Pranayama without precise timing, bandha engagement, or structural body readiness.

Conflicting Diet & Lifestyle

Consuming non-sattvic foods or irregular meal timings that actively counteract morning practices.

Solitary Doubts

Experiencing strange meditative sensations or physical roadblocks without an experienced teacher to offer context.

Lack of Accountability

Difficulty maintaining daily discipline amid modern work pressures, family responsibilities, and distractions.
